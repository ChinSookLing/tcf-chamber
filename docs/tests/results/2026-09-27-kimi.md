## Site problems found (confirmed by Opus)

1. **Text index numbering ≠ chamber IDs (real, most serious).** `chambers/index.html` is an `<ol>` sorted newest first, so the visible numbers 1–156 run opposite to ch156–ch001, and the chNNN id is not shown. A reader looking for "078" finds the wrong work.
2. **Record rows run together in plain-text extraction (real).** `<dt>Image made with</dt><dd>drawn by GPT…` becomes "Image made withdrawn by GPT…". Not a typo in the data; a separator is missing between label and value.
3. **Per-chamber dates (minor).** Start Here tells the timeline by chamber dates; a reader may think per-chamber publication dates exist. They are creation dates; publication per chamber is not recorded.
